BUSINESS / CIRCULAR THINKING

Circular Economy Workshop, India.

Bridging traditional wisdom and modern sustainability in the heart of India.

Workshop Overview

This special edition of our business workshop series was held in India, focusing on the principles of the Circular Economy. We worked with students to analyze local waste streams and develop business concepts that turn "waste" into value. By combining modern economic theories with India's rich history of resourcefulness, students created innovative solutions for their local communities.

Workshop Impact

By bringing together youth from across the region, this workshop empowered participants to rethink waste as a resource. Students developed concepts for local circular businesses, bridging traditional practices with modern sustainability models.

Lesson Plan Overview

Topic: Circular Economy Fundamentals

  • Introduction: Defining Linear vs. Circular Economy. Understanding the value of resources.
  • Case Studies: Examining local examples of circularity (e.g., composting, upcycling).
  • Interactive Activity: "Waste Audit" - Categorizing waste and brainstorming value-added products.
  • Group Project: Designing a sustainable business model canvas.
