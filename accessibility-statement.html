Website Policies

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Our Commitment

Ethos Sustainability is committed to making this website accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability. We aim to follow recognized accessibility best practices and improve the experience over time.

What We Try To Support

  • Keyboard navigation and visible focus states.
  • Readable text contrast and responsive layouts.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images where practical.
  • Clear form labels and accessible controls.

Known Limitations

Some older or third-party content may not fully meet accessibility expectations. We work to identify and reduce those barriers as we maintain the site.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please let us know what page or feature is affected and what assistive technology you are using, if you are comfortable sharing it. We will review the issue and do our best to help.
