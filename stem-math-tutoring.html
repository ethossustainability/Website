Math Tutoring at ILT Keller K-8

Empowering 4th graders with foundational math skills

Project Overview

In this outreach project, Ethos volunteers taught 4th grade math at ILT Keller K-8, helping students build foundational skills and confidence. The program focused on interactive lessons and personalized support, making math accessible and fun for young learners.
