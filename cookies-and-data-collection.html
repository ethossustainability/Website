Cookies & Data Collection

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Overview

Ethos Sustainability uses limited data collection to operate the website, respond to messages, and improve the experience for visitors. This page explains what we collect and how cookies or similar technologies may be used.

What We Collect

  • Information you submit through forms, such as your name, email address, and message.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device information, and page activity that may be recorded by hosting or embedded services.
  • Any information you choose to share with us directly by email or other contact methods.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for the site to function, remember preferences, or support embedded third-party services. If you interact with those services, they may set their own cookies or collect data under their own policies.

How We Use Data

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested information.
  • To maintain and improve the website.
  • To understand general site usage and troubleshoot technical issues.

Your Choices

You can usually control cookies through your browser settings. You may also choose not to submit information through our forms, although that may limit our ability to respond to your request.

Contact

If you have a question about cookies or data collection, email info@ethossustainability.org.
