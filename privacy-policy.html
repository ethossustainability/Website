Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Ethos Sustainability handles information collected through this website, including forms, email communication, and third-party services used to host or support the site.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit voluntarily, such as your name and email address.
  • Message content or other information you choose to share with us.
  • Basic technical data that may be collected by the website host or embedded services.

How We Use Information

  • To reply to your questions and requests.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website.
  • To monitor for spam, abuse, or technical issues.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help host the site, process form submissions, or support other website functions. Those providers are expected to use the information only to perform services for us.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to respond to inquiries, support our programs, or meet legal and operational needs. If you would like us to remove a message or contact detail, you can reach out using the contact information below.
